Privacy Policy for Memorize

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Memorize is a memory matching game developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Personal Information

Memorize does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to the developer.

Game progress and related game data are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Memorize uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and information related to ad interactions for purposes including delivering, measuring, and improving advertising.

Where required by applicable law, you may be asked for consent before certain advertising-related data processing takes place.

Third-Party Services

Memorize uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

Children

Memorize is a general-audience memory game and is not specifically directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or services change. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the support information provided on the Memorize App Store page.
